# Global proof hypotheses and parameter strata

The affine rank mapping route proves full-real affine maps only. Real finite matrix coefficients and offsets must be established before its real mapping conclusions apply. Restricted codomains are not ignored by bijectivity; unsupported ones decline. Rank over complex scalars alone is insufficient for real surjectivity. Conditional matrix reality remains a coverage limit.

Real injective-outer and affine-output closures recurse into an inner real map only when that inner expression is real throughout the relevant support. This prevents complex exponential compositions from shrinking their inner domain. Finite injectivity uses original point values and original pole guards. A constant map is noninjective only after at least two distinct domain points are proved; missing evidence of singleton size is not a proof of noninjectivity.

Finite monotonicity uses exact ordered point values before registry or derivative rules. Derivative sign changes alone are not order witnesses on isolated points or arbitrary disconnected sets. Genuine partition and exact ordering witnesses remain available for negative conclusions.

Negative polynomial periodicity and symmetry proofs require surviving coefficients under the applicable parameter information. Failure of a symbolic zero identity in the joint variables and parameters does not prove the claimed negative on every parameter stratum. Incommensurate-period negatives use the audited case of two genuine nonzero harmonic terms with a proved irrational frequency ratio; merely lacking a discovered common period is insufficient. Compact conditional frequency queries return UNKNOWN when their guard is unresolved; rich records retain the guarded theorem and can be replayed.

Reflection centers must be finite real points. Replay checks discovered center families, their periods and supplied candidate centers, not only the primary reflection identity. Symmetry and periodicity remain expression-identity analyses; stored domain information should not be interpreted as a general proof that an arbitrary restricted domain is invariant under reflections or shifts.

Requested nonpolynomial homogeneity degrees retain possible zero multipliers. Unresolved parameter families decline unsupported negative conclusions. Results retain requested_degree separately from an inferred degree or alternate degree cases, so replay does not reconstruct the wrong query. This is a targeted record extension, not a package-wide result-schema migration.
